Privacy Policy

Kagehinata (the "App") respects your privacy. This page explains what information the App handles and how.

Information we collect

The developer of the App does not collect any personal information. There is no account and no server run by the developer.

Information used only on your device

  • Location: used only with your permission, to show the sun direction and shadows for where you are.
  • Motion sensor (compass): used only after you tap the compass button, to know which way your phone is facing.
  • Saved places and settings: stored only on your device and removed when you delete the App.

Information sent to third-party services

Some features send the minimum information needed to the services below. The developer does not receive or store this information. Please see each service's privacy policy for how they handle it.

InformationServicePurpose
Approximate coordinates of the selected place (about 1 km)Open-MeteoWeather forecast
The map area being shownOpenFreeMap, GSI JapanMap display
Coordinates and area around the center of the zoomed-in mapGSI Japan (municipality lookup), PLATEAU (MLIT)Building shadows
Search text (online search only)GSI Japan, OpenStreetMap NominatimPlace search

Nothing is sent while you are offline; the App then works with bundled data.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The App contains no ads or analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The App can be used by people of all ages and collects no information about children.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
