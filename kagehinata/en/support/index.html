Support

Kagehinata (かげひなた) shows the sun's direction and height, how long shadows fall, and the shadows of nearby buildings for any place and time you pick on the map. The interface is in Japanese.

FAQ

My location is not shown

Please allow location access for Kagehinata in the iPhone Settings app. If you do not, the app starts at Sapporo Station. Tap the map to choose any place.

How do I go back to my location?

Tap the arrow button at the top right of the map. The blue dot is where you are, and the faint circle shows how far off it may be. Right after opening the app the position may be slightly off; it settles within a few seconds.

Building shadows are not shown

Zoom in on the map. An internet connection is needed. The app uses PLATEAU 3D city models in Japan and OpenStreetMap buildings elsewhere, so shadows may not appear where there is no data. Also check that building shadows are turned on in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sun calculations, place search and a simple map work with bundled data. Weather comments, detailed maps and building shadows need a connection.

The compass is off

Metal or magnets nearby can affect it. Moving the phone in a figure-eight may help calibrate it.
