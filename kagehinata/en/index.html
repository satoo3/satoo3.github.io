Kagehinata

Check sunlight on the map

An iPhone app that shows the sun's direction and height and the shadows of nearby buildings

View on the App Store

Features

  • The sun and shadows at a glanceTap the map to see the sun's direction and height and which way and how far shadows fall.
  • Shadows of nearby buildingsZoom in to see building shadows on the map, whether a spot is in sun or shade, and which hours it gets sunlight. You can compare sunlight with and without buildings.
  • Any day of the yearChange the time and date with sliders, or jump to the equinoxes and solstices.
  • The sky from where you standToday's sun path and the figure-eight of the sun at the same time over a year. The sky color follows the time and cloud cover.
  • Friendly commentsHow long your shadow is, how well a solar panel would charge, and more, based on the weather forecast.
  • Works offlineSun calculations, place search and a simple map work with bundled data.

The app interface is in Japanese. Sun positions are astronomical estimates. Building shadows are approximate, based on open data (PLATEAU in Japan, OpenStreetMap elsewhere).
