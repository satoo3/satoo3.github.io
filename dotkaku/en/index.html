Dot Kaku

Draw pixel art and animated GIFs with your finger

A pixel art and animation app for iPhone, in English and Japanese (English from version 1.5).

View on the App Store

Features

  • Pixel art on a gridDraw with pen, fill, line and eraser. Pinch to zoom in.
  • Flip-book animationsStack frames to make animated pixel art.
  • Send as GIF, video or imageSend to LINE, X, Instagram, TikTok and more, scaled up with every pixel crisp. For apps that don't accept GIFs, send a square or vertical video.
  • iCloud syncYour sketches sync automatically with your other iPhones signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Onion skin and major grid linesSee the previous and next frames faintly, and draw thicker grid lines every few cells.
  • Import from other appsShare images and animated GIFs from Photos and other apps to Dot Kaku.
