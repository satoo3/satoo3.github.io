Support

Dot Kaku (ドット描く) is a pixel art app. Draw with your finger, make flip-book style animations, and send them as an animated GIF, a video (MP4) or an image (PNG) to apps like LINE, X, Instagram and TikTok. Your sketches sync with your other iPhones through iCloud (from version 1.5). The app is available in English and Japanese.

FAQ

How do I send my art to social and messaging apps?

Tap the share button at the top right of the editor and choose "Send as GIF", "Send as Square Video", "Send as Vertical Video" or "Send as Image (PNG)". The share sheet opens so you can pick an app. Our recommendations:

  • LINE, X, Slack, Discord: animated GIF. In LINE, tap the GIF in the chat to play it.
  • Instagram, TikTok: vertical video. Neither app posts animated GIFs as moving images.

To choose a size, or to get a GIF with a transparent background, individual frames or a Zip file, use "Choose Size & Format" in the share menu.

The app I want is far down in the share sheet

iOS orders the apps in the share sheet and shows the ones you use most first. To keep an app at the front, tap "More" at the end of the app row, tap "Edit", add the app to Favorites and drag it to the top.

How do I save my art to Photos?

Choose a format from the share button, then tap "Save Image" ("Save Video" for videos) in the share sheet. Allow adding to Photos the first time. You can change this later in Settings > Apps > ドット描く > Photos.

I can't find my GIF in Photos

Look in Photos > Collections > Media Types > Animated. Thumbnails look still, but the GIF plays when you open it. Animated GIF is available for sketches with two or more frames.

My art looks tiny on social media

Choose the "For Social Media" size when sharing. It scales your art up to about 1024 pixels on the long side while keeping the pixels crisp. Some apps may show animated GIFs as still images.

Can I import images from other apps?

Yes. Share an image from Photos or another app and choose ドット描く. It appears in your sketch list the next time you open the app. Large images are scaled down to 256 pixels on the long side, and animated GIFs are imported frame by frame.

How do I sync my sketches with my other iPhones?

Sketches sync automatically between iPhones signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account (from version 1.5). Your art, frames and settings such as playback speed are synced. Undo history stays on each device and isn't synced. If you're not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is turned off for Dot Kaku in Settings, your sketches are saved only on your device, as before. A notice appears at the top of the screen when changes arrive from another device.

What are onion skin and major grid lines?

Toggle them with the buttons at the top of the editor. Onion skin shows the previous frame in red and the next frame in blue, faintly, to help you draw motion. Major grid lines draw thicker lines every few cells. Touch and hold either button to change its settings.

How do I undo?

Use the left arrow to undo and the right arrow to redo.
