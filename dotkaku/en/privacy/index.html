Privacy Policy

Dot Kaku (ドット描く, "the App") respects your privacy. This page explains what information the App handles and how.

Information we collect

The developer does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no developer-run servers, and no advertising or analytics tools.

Information the App stores

  • Your sketches: stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also stored in your own iCloud account (an Apple service) and synced with your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Data in iCloud is stored only in your account, and the developer can't see it. You can turn off iCloud for the App in the Settings app. Undo history is stored only on your device.
  • Photos: the App adds your art to your photo library only when you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet. It never reads photos in your library.
  • Images shared from other apps: images you share to ドット描く are imported as sketches on your device, and the received files are deleted after import.

Information sent outside your device

The App does not send your information to the developer or any external service. iCloud sync happens only between the App and your own account through Apple's service. The App works offline, and syncs when you're connected. When you use the share feature, images are passed to the app you choose (such as messaging or social apps). Please see each app's or service's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Changes
